# Changelog

## 2026-11-23
- **A second page for each category** (Ayarlar → Görünüm → **İkinci sayfa**, off until you turn it on). Each category gets 12 more places. Temel, Eylem, Günlük, Vücut and Eşyalar were full, so now words like İç, Ye, Uyu, Büyük, Küçük can go into their own category.
  - On the board, **tap the chosen category again** to see its 2nd page; tap once more to go back. Two small dots on the category button show which page is open.
  - **The first page does not change at all**, and a category always opens on its first page. A category whose 2nd page is still empty works exactly as before.
  - Kelime ekle puts a new word on the 2nd page only when the first page is full. In the Kelimeler list, 2nd-page words appear under a "2. sayfa" heading.
  - Turning the option off keeps those words; they just stop being shown.

## 2026-11-22 (more word forms)
- **Kelime çekimleri: "o yaptı" too.** Every action now also has the third-person past: **geldi, gitti, aldı, verdi, getirdi, baktı, oynadı, izledi, aradı, bekledi, yaptı, gönderdi.**
- **Action words added from Hazır resimler have forms too:** İç (içiyorum, içtim, içti, içsin, içme), Ye, Otur, Kalk, Dur, Uyu, Sarıl, Aç, Kapat.
- All new forms are recorded in Ahmet's voice.

## 2026-11-22 (faster speech)
- **A tapped word is spoken at once.** It used to wait until the picture had flown into the balloon (about 0.6 s), and the voice clip was loaded at that moment. Now the word is said the moment it is tapped, while the picture flies, and the clips of every word on screen are kept ready in memory.

## 2026-11-22 (iPhone)
- **Sesim Var now has its own iPhone layout** instead of a shrunken iPad screen. Upright: the balloon spans the width, with Oku, Gönder and the small buttons in a row under it, and the board shows 3 × 4 cards (the same 12 places in the same order). Sideways: everything in one compact screen. The category bar scrolls sideways. Cards grow or shrink to fit the phone, and Düğme boyutu still works.
- The app keeps clear of the iPhone's notch and home bar.
- **Ayarlar on iPhone** is one scrolling page (two columns when sideways).
- iPad looks exactly as before.
- The voice is now the one the family picked: Ahmet, "daha genç" (a bit higher and quicker).

## 2026-11-22 (voice)
- **A young man's voice reads the board.** Every built-in word, ready-made picture and word form (346 in all) is now prerecorded in a young male Turkish voice, "Ahmet". It is the default and sounds the same on every iPad and iPhone, offline too. Konuşma hızı (Yavaş / Normal / Hızlı) works with it.
- Ayarlar → Ses ve papağan → Okuyan ses: choose Ahmet or a device voice. Words the family types themselves are still read by the device voice for now. A word with a family recording still plays that recording; delete the recording in Ayarlar to hear Ahmet instead.

## 2026-11-22 (Kişiler)
- **Kişiler: Barış, Güler Teyze and Dayı are on the board.** Güler Teyze takes the place of the hidden "Teyze" card, Dayı is shown again, and Barış goes in the next free place. Nothing else moves. Each has forms when held (Barış'a, Güler Teyze'ye, dayıma…). Barış and Güler Teyze use a placeholder picture for now: Ayarlar → the word → Fotoğraf to give them a real photo.
- One-time board changes like this one now run only once, so a word hidden again later stays hidden.

## 2026-11-22 (morning)
- **12 new everyday words** in the ready-made pictures, each with a new picture: **İç, Ye, Otur, Kalk, Dur, Uyu, Sarıl, Büyük, Küçük, Çok, Az, Soğuk.** Type them in Kelime ekle, or pick them from the row.
- **The ready-made pictures follow the chosen category:** choose Eylem and Eylem's pictures come first.

## 2026-11-22
- **Every picture is now in the new outlined style:** Eşyalar, Duygu, Ekran, Yerler and all the category icons, 121 pictures in total.
- **More words on the board.** Ekran: Oyun, YouTube, Netflix, Film, Dizi, Çizgi film, Haberler. Yerler: Köy, Elazığ. If the app is already installed, these appear by themselves wherever their place is free. Nothing that was there moves.
- **32 more ready-made pictures in Kelime ekle:** Göz, Kulak, El, Boğaz, Sırt, Ayak, Kan, Vurdum, Öksürük, Ateş, Hastalandı, İyi, Üzgün, Sıkıldım, Seviyorum, Özledim, Güzel, Korktum, Kızgın, İnternet, Müzik, Video, Araba, Para, Hastane, Park, Market, Dışarı, Kayboldu, and more.
- Every word from the first version is now in the app, either on the board or in the ready-made pictures.
- Word forms for the new words too: YouTube'da, filmi, köye, Elazığ'a…

## 2026-11-21 (late night)
- **Kelime çekimleri** (Ayarlar → Görünüm, off by default): hold a card to choose a form of the word. Examples: **kolum, kollarım, koluma** (so "Kolum acıyor" works), **suyu, suya, suda**, **annem, anneme, annemle**, and for actions **geliyorum, geldim, gelsin, gelme**. All 45 built-in words that have forms were written by hand, so irregular ones are right (karın → karnım, su → suyu). A single tap works exactly as before. The chosen form is spoken, sent in Messages and kept in Son cümleler.
- **Hazır resimler** in Kelime ekle: the ready-made pictures can be browsed without typing; tap one to fill in the word.

## 2026-11-21 (night)
- **Gönder now sends a message.** In Ayarlar → **Gönder → Mesajlar**, enter the phone number or iMessage address of the person who should receive messages. After that, Gönder opens Messages with the sentence written and addressed to them; one tap on the send arrow sends it. "Deneme mesajı aç" tries it out. If the address is left empty, Gönder speaks the sentence as before.
- **Oku and Gönder line up with the speech balloon** in landscape (they used to sit lower than it).

## 2026-11-21 (evening)
- **Son cümleler:** a new clock button in the speech balloon shows the last 12 sentences that were read or sent. Tap one and it comes back into the balloon and is read aloud. "Temizle" clears the list. It stays on this device only, and is included in backups.
- **Yanlışlıkla dokunma koruması** (Ayarlar → Görünüm): when turned on, taps that come within half a second or one second after a card are ignored. It helps when hands are unsteady. Off by default.
- **Konuşma hızı** (Ayarlar → Ses ve papağan): Yavaş, Normal or Hızlı. Choosing one plays a short sample. Family recordings always play at their own speed.
